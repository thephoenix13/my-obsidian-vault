# Trading / Investment Discussion — Personal Reference

> Context: A friend is offering 36% in 12 months / 40% in 13 months on stock-market investments, apart from his own profit. Notes below capture what to understand about the structure and what documents make sense to review before committing capital — standard due diligence I'd do for any investment of this size.

---

## Context on returns

For reference points:
- Warren Buffett's lifetime CAGR: ~20%
- Nifty 50 long-term average: ~12-14%
- Top-performing Indian mutual funds: 15-18% over long periods
- SEBI-registered PMS managers: 18-25% is considered exceptional

Understanding how the 36-40% number is generated, and what risk sits behind it, is the main thing to explore in conversation.

---

## Documents to review

### 1. Regulatory & legal identity

| Document | What to verify |
|---|---|
| SEBI Portfolio Manager (PMS) registration certificate | Number starts with `INP0000xxxxx`. Verify at sebi.gov.in → Intermediaries → Portfolio Managers |
| SEBI Investment Adviser (RIA) registration | Number starts with `INA0000xxxxx`. Verify on SEBI site |
| SEBI Research Analyst registration (if he provides trade calls) | Number starts with `INH0000xxxxx` |
| AMFI ARN (if routing through mutual funds) | Verify at amfiindia.com |
| Company registration | CIN from MCA portal (mca.gov.in) — incorporation date, paid-up capital, directors, charges |
| GST registration | GSTIN — verify on gst.gov.in |
| PAN of the entity and his personal PAN | — |
| Trade license / Shop & Establishment certificate | — |

### 2. Track record

| Document | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Audited financial statements — last 5 years | P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow. Signed by a CA with membership number. Verify CA on icai.org |
| Income Tax Returns (ITR) — last 5 years with computation | Cross-check declared income with claimed returns |
| Form 26AS / AIS | Shows TDS and capital gains reported to IT department |
| Audited PMS performance disclosure | If PMS-registered, SEBI mandates monthly disclosure in a standard format |
| Demat account statements (CDSL/NSDL) — last 3-5 years | Actual holdings. CAS can be downloaded directly from nsdl.co.in / cdslindia.com |
| Broker contract notes — sample months across years | Sourced from the broker (Zerodha, ICICI Direct, etc.) |
| Bank statements of the business entity — last 2-3 years | Helps understand inflows and outflows of the trading operation |

### 3. How the money is handled

| Document | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Investment agreement | Read every clause: who holds the money, signing authority, treatment of losses, exit clause, dispute resolution jurisdiction |
| Where the demat account sits | Best practice for PMS is a demat in my name with him holding Power of Attorney to trade. Worth understanding the structure he uses |
| Power of Attorney draft (if applicable) | Should be limited to trading, not withdrawals or transfers |
| Custodian details | PMS arrangements typically use a SEBI-registered custodian (HDFC, Kotak Mahindra, Orbis, etc.) |
| Risk disclosure document | SEBI-mandated for PMS |
| Fee structure document | Management fee + performance fee, clearly stated |

### 4. References & existing clients

| Document | Use |
|---|---|
| List of 5-10 existing clients with phone numbers | Speak with them about their experience — duration invested, withdrawal experience, any delays |
| Withdrawal track record | Useful to understand how full redemptions have worked historically — one or two real examples |

### 5. The trading itself

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Trading strategy in writing | F&O, equity delivery, algo, arbitrage — understand the approach |
| Sample trading logs with timestamps | Match against exchange data (NSE/BSE historical) for sanity |
| Algo/strategy SEBI approval | If algo trading, SEBI now requires registration |
| Insurance / Professional Indemnity | What's covered if something goes wrong operationally |

### 6. Tax & compliance

- Form 16A / TDS certificates — what's the tax treatment of the returns? Capital gains, business income, or interest? Get it in writing
- Will gains be reflected in my AIS / Form 26AS?
- GST on his service fee — should be charged and shown

## Pre-meeting verification

1. **SEBI intermediary check** — sebi.gov.in → "Intermediaries / Market Infrastructure Institutions" → search his name
2. **MCA company check** — mca.gov.in → "View Company/LLP Master Data"
3. **Search his name + the entity name** on Google for any public history
4. **SEBI orders** — sebi.gov.in → "Enforcement" → search his name

---

## Questions to discuss in the meeting

1. **"If I invest ₹X today and need to withdraw on day 30, how does that work?"**
   Understand the lock-in, exit terms, and any penalties.

2. **"Can I speak to a client who has completed a full cycle and withdrawn principal + returns?"**
   A real reference conversation is more useful than any document.

3. **"Will the trading account be in my name with my PAN, and will my demat reflect every trade?"**
   Clarifies the legal structure of the arrangement.

4. **"How are the 36-40% returns generated, and what's the historical drawdown?"**
   Helps understand the strategy and downside risk, not just upside.

---

## Alternatives to keep in mind for comparison

- **SIP into index funds or quality mutual funds** — 12-15% CAGR over 10-15 years. Boring, regulated, compounds.
- **SEBI-registered PMS** — minimum ₹50 lakh ticket, regulated, audited, cannot promise fixed returns but can target 18-25%.

Good to know what the "boring" benchmark looks like so the conversation has context.
