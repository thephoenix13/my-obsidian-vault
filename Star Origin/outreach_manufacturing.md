## Industry Context

Manufacturing companies face a dual hiring challenge: they need both blue-collar shop floor talent and white-collar engineering, operations, and management professionals — often at the same time and at scale. For startups setting up greenfield facilities and mid-size firms expanding capacity, the stakes of getting hiring wrong are directly tied to production output.

**Key hiring challenges in Manufacturing:**
- Simultaneous need for floor staff, supervisors, engineers, and plant managers
- Bulk hiring requirements tied to production ramp-ups
- High attrition in shop floor and contract workforce
- Difficulty sourcing specialized roles (quality control, industrial engineers, safety officers)
- Labour law compliance and statutory requirement awareness in candidates
- Seasonal and project-based workforce fluctuation
