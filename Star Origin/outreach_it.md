## Industry Context

The IT sector faces one of the most competitive talent markets globally. Startups are racing to build engineering teams before competitors. Mid-size firms are battling high attrition, skill obsolescence, and rising salary benchmarks. The war for tech talent is relentless — and traditional hiring methods simply don't keep pace.

**Key hiring challenges in IT:**
- Extremely high demand for niche skills (cloud, AI/ML, DevOps, cybersecurity)
- Candidate-driven market — top engineers have multiple offers
- Long notice periods delay project starts
- High attrition rates (especially 12–18 months post-join)
- Difficulty assessing technical depth without domain expertise in-house
- US and global staffing requirements for IT services companies
