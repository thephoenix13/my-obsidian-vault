## Additional Recommendations

| Area | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| **Segmentation** | Split list by industry/company size for personalized subject lines |
| **Personalization** | Use `[First Name]`, company name, and industry where possible |
| **Send Time** | Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10 AM or 1–3 PM recipient timezone |
| **Tracking** | Monitor open rate, click rate, and reply rate per email |
| **A/B Test** | Test subject lines on Emails 1, 3, and 8 first |
| **CRM** | Tag responders immediately and move them to a sales sequence |
| **Unsubscribe** | Always include a clean unsubscribe link (CAN-SPAM / GDPR compliance) |
