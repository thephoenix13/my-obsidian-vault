## Industry Context

Fintech companies operate at the intersection of financial services and technology — making talent one of their most critical competitive advantages. Whether it's a Series A startup building its core product team or a mid-size payment platform scaling its compliance and engineering functions, the pressure to hire fast and hire right is constant.

**Key hiring challenges in Fintech:**
- Scarcity of candidates who understand both tech and financial regulation
- Compliance, risk, and AML roles require highly specialized knowledge
- Engineering talent is heavily competed for by banks, big tech, and other fintechs
- Rapid scaling after funding rounds strains small HR teams
- High attrition in sales and customer success functions
- Background verification is non-negotiable in regulated finance roles
