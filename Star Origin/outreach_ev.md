## Industry Context

The EV sector in India and globally is in a high-growth, high-pressure phase. Companies are racing to build products, scale manufacturing, and capture market share — all while competing for a very thin pool of talent that understands battery technology, embedded systems, power electronics, and EV-specific software. For startups with aggressive timelines and mid-size OEMs scaling production, hiring is a mission-critical bottleneck.

**Key hiring challenges in EV:**
- Extremely niche talent pool (battery engineers, BMS developers, power electronics experts)
- Cross-disciplinary roles requiring both mechanical and electrical/software expertise
- Competition for talent from legacy auto OEMs, big tech, and global EV players
- Fast-changing technology landscape making skills obsolescence a real risk
- Need for both R&D talent (innovation) and manufacturing talent (scale) simultaneously
- Policy and regulatory knowledge (FAME, AIS, CMVR) increasingly required
