## Industry Context

B2B SaaS companies live and die by their go-to-market execution and product quality — both of which depend entirely on the quality of people behind them. Startups need to hire their first AEs, SDRs, and product managers at the right moment. Mid-size SaaS companies are scaling customer success, solutions engineering, and growth functions while managing high sales team attrition. In both cases, the wrong hire — or a role that stays open too long — directly impacts ARR.

**Key hiring challenges in B2B SaaS:**
- High attrition in sales (especially SDRs and AEs in the first year)
- Difficulty finding candidates who understand complex B2B sales cycles
- Specialized roles (solutions engineers, RevOps, customer success) are thin in supply
- First sales hires are disproportionately high-stakes — they set culture and process
- Product and engineering talent competes with funded startups and big tech
- Employer brand is often underdeveloped at startup/early mid-size stage
