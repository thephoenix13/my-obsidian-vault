# Consulting Proposal — Clean Electric
### People Architecture for a Hypergrowth EV Battery Company

---

## Intelligence Brief — Who Is Clean Electric?

**Clean Electric** (Pune, founded 2020) is one of India's most technically differentiated EV battery startups. Built by three IIT graduates — Akash Gupta (CEO), Abhinav Roy (CTO), and Ankit Joshi (CPO) — the company has developed a **patented immersion cooling technology (0Kelvin™)** that enables sub-15-minute EV charging, a first in India at mass market price points.

**Where they are today:**
- **$8.35M raised** — Seed (Kalaari, Climate Angels, 2022) + Series A ($6M, Info Edge Ventures, Pi Ventures, Kalaari, Lok Capital — August 2024)
- **6,000+ batteries deployed** across 100+ locations, 20M+ km covered
- **12 active OEM customers** (Bounce Infinity, Omega Seiki Mobility, and others) with 8 more in pipeline
- **Revenue:** INR 59L (FY23) → INR 2.3 Cr (FY24) → INR 8.92 Cr (FY25) — **294% CAGR**
- **Team:** 60–125 employees (164% YoY headcount growth post Series A)
- **Manufacturing scale-up:** 20 MWh → 150–200 MWh annual capacity underway
- **4 patents granted** (India + US) on immersion cooling architecture

**Their ambition (stated publicly):** *"Build a hardware-centric global tech company from India."* — Akash Gupta, Forbes India Podcast

---

## The Real Situation

Clean Electric has built something genuinely impressive: patented technology, institutional backing, real OEM deployments, and explosive revenue growth. They are not a slide-deck startup.

But they are at the most dangerous stage for a hardware company — **post-Series A hypergrowth** — where product and funding have outpaced the organization's ability to execute at scale.

The signals are clear:

| Signal | What It Means |
|---|---|
| 164% YoY headcount growth | Processes, culture, and management are all under strain |
| No visible HR function | A 60–125 person team with no People Ops is a ticking clock |
| Three 27–30-year-old founders running everything | Founder bottleneck — no management layer between them and engineers |
| 7.5–10x manufacturing capacity scale-up | Industrial workforce management, quality systems, and production leadership are all stress-tested simultaneously |
| Hiring only 5–10 year experienced engineers | Premium talent requires premium people practices to retain |
| INR 100 Cr ARR target in 12 months | An 11x revenue jump requires sales process maturity, not just founder hustle |
| CGO (Sachin Singh, ex-Tata AutoComp) just hired | Signals a pivot to institutional OEM sales — needs a team and a process behind him |
| Empty blog, 150 Instagram followers | Zero employer brand investment — a liability when competing for top engineering talent |

This is where **Perfect Skills** comes in.

## Proposed Engagement — Six Workstreams

The following is a phased, integrated consulting engagement tailored to Clean Electric's current stage, team profile, and growth ambitions. It goes well beyond a standard training program.

---

### Workstream 1 — Performance Management Foundation
**What:** KPI & Goal Setting Framework across all functions
**Who:** Engineering leads, Manufacturing leads, Software team, Sales/BD
**Format:** 2-Day Facilitated Workshop + Framework Document

Clean Electric's three founders are running engineering, product, and operations simultaneously. There is almost certainly no standardized KPI framework — goals are set informally, performance is assessed subjectively, and high performers have no structured path to recognition.

**Deliverables:**
- KPI Design Reference Sheet tailored to EV battery manufacturing (cycle yield, deployment SLA, BMS uptime, OEM satisfaction scores, software release cadence)
- SMART Goal Writing with Clean Electric-specific examples
- OKR cascade methodology — from company ARR targets down to team-level KPIs
- Goal Review Rhythm Guide (weekly standups, monthly reviews, quarterly OKR check-ins)
- KPI Maturity Checklist for self-assessment per team

**Why now:** With 12 OEM customers and an 11x revenue target, every team needs to know exactly what they're responsible for delivering — and how it connects to the company's growth.

---

### Workstream 2 — Organizational Design & Management Layer
**What:** Org structure design + role architecture for the next phase of growth
**Who:** Founders + HR (if exists) + functional heads
**Format:** Consulting Engagement (2–3 weeks)

Clean Electric has three brilliant founders and a growing team — but no visible management layer in between. This is a structural bottleneck that will slow decision-making, create founder burnout, and limit the company's ability to scale.

**Deliverables:**
- Current org structure mapping and gap analysis
- Recommended org design for 100–200 person engineering + manufacturing company
- Role definitions for critical mid-management positions (Engineering Manager, Manufacturing Operations Lead, BMS Software Lead, Sales Operations Manager)
- Accountability framework — who owns what, at what level
- Delegation matrix — what founders should own vs. hand off
- RACI charts for cross-functional processes (OEM onboarding, product launch, quality escalation)

**Why now:** Post-Series A is exactly when founder-led structures break down. Clean Electric has 12–24 months to build a management layer before scale makes it painful.

---

### Workstream 3 — People Function Setup
**What:** Build the HR/People Operations function from scratch
**Who:** Founding team + any HR hire they bring in
**Format:** Consulting + Implementation Support (4–6 weeks)

Clean Electric has no visible HR function despite being a 60–125 person company that grew 164% in a year. This means onboarding is ad hoc, policies don't exist, and the burden falls on founders and team leads.

**Deliverables:**
- HR Policies & Employee Handbook (joining, leave, code of conduct, performance, exit)
- Onboarding & Induction Program — structured 30/60/90-day plan for new hires
- HR SOP Library — hiring process, offer letters, appraisal cycle, resignation handling
- HR Calendar — annual rhythm of performance reviews, increment cycles, engagement touchpoints
- Tools & Systems Recommendations — HRMS setup guidance (Keka, Zoho People, or equivalent)
- Job Description Library — standardized JDs for all current and planned roles

**Why now:** Every week without these systems means inconsistent experiences for new hires, founder time wasted on admin, and growing compliance risk.

---

### Workstream 4 — Compensation & Career Architecture
**What:** Pay band structure + designation framework + career ladders
**Who:** Founders + HR
**Format:** Consulting Document + Reference Framework

Clean Electric is hiring experienced professionals (5–10 years) in a competitive Pune market for hardware, manufacturing, and software talent. Without structured compensation bands and career ladders, they will lose offers to Tata Motors, Ather Energy, and Ola Electric — all of whom have structured HR practices.

**Deliverables:**
- **Pay Band Design** — market-benchmarked salary ranges across all levels and functions (R&D, Manufacturing, Software/BMS, Sales, Operations, Product)
- **Designation Rationalization** — clean hierarchy from Associate → Senior → Lead → Manager → Senior Manager → Director
- **Career Ladder** — transparent growth paths within each function (Engineering Track, Manufacturing Track, Software Track, Business Track)
- **Variable Pay & Incentive Guidelines** — linking performance ratings to increments and bonuses
- **Joining Offer Benchmarks** — competitive ranges for the 5–10 year experienced profiles they're actively hiring
- **EV Sector Benchmarking** — compensation positioned against Ather, Ola Electric, Exicom, Neuron Energy

**Why now:** They're in active hiring mode for senior profiles. Every unstructured compensation conversation is a risk.

---

### Workstream 5 — Leadership Development for Founders & Emerging Managers
**What:** Structured leadership capability building for the founding team and first management layer
**Who:** 3 Co-Founders + 5–8 emerging team leads
**Format:** Monthly Coaching Sessions + Workshop (3–6 months)

Akash, Abhinav, and Ankit are 27–30 years old, running a funded hardware company for the first time. They are brilliant engineers and entrepreneurs — but leading at scale requires a different set of skills: delegation, giving feedback, managing managers, cross-functional alignment, and building culture deliberately.

**Deliverables:**
- **Founder Leadership Assessment** — strengths, blind spots, working styles
- **Leadership Workshop** — Moving from "doing" to "leading": delegation, accountability, feedback culture
- **First-Time Manager Program** — for team leads being elevated to management roles (how to run 1:1s, set goals, handle underperformance, build team culture)
- **Monthly Coaching Cadence** — structured sessions with the founding team over 3–6 months to embed frameworks and work through real challenges
- **Decision-Making Framework** — what decisions founders make vs. delegate vs. involve the team in

**Why now:** Sachin Singh's hiring signals they're adding experienced industry veterans. Managing someone with 20+ years of experience (Sachin is ex-Tata AutoComp, SPJIMR, Carnegie Mellon) requires founders to step up as leaders — not just as engineers.

---

### Workstream 6 — Sales Team Capability & OEM Account Management
**What:** Sales process design + capability building for the commercial team
**Who:** CGO Sachin Singh + sales/BD team
**Format:** Workshop + Process Documentation (2 weeks)

Clean Electric has 12 OEM customers and 8 in the pipeline. Moving from founder-led sales to a scalable OEM sales engine is their most critical commercial challenge. Sachin Singh's hire is the right move — but he needs a team, a process, and a playbook behind him.

**Deliverables:**
- **OEM Sales Process Design** — from lead qualification to technical demonstration to pilot to commercial contract
- **Sales Capability Workshop** — B2B enterprise selling, OEM account management, technical selling (for engineers who interface with OEM teams)
- **Account Management Framework** — how to grow existing OEM relationships (Bounce, Omega Seiki) from 1 product to full fleet partnerships
- **Sales KPI Framework** — pipeline metrics, conversion rates, OEM satisfaction scores, revenue per account
- **Proposal & Presentation Skills** — structured training for the team on pitching to OEM procurement and leadership teams
- **CRM Adoption Support** — guidance on implementing a CRM (HubSpot or Zoho) and building pipeline discipline

**Why now:** 8 OEMs in advanced discussions means the sales team is under immediate pressure. A structured process will close deals faster and at higher contract values.

## Why Clean Electric Needs This Now

**The 12–24 month window is critical.** Here is what happens to hypergrowth hardware startups that don't build people systems:

- Top engineers leave because there's no career path or compensation clarity
- OEM deals close slower because the sales team has no process
- Manufacturing quality suffers because production teams have no structured KPIs or accountability
- Founders burn out because there's no management layer to absorb decisions
- New hires onboard poorly and take 6+ months to become productive
- Culture becomes accidental instead of intentional — and at 100+ people, accidental culture is usually a bad culture

Neuron Energy (Clean Electric's closest Pune-based competitor) just opened a 1.5 GWh facility and is scaling fast. Clean Electric's differentiation window on technology is 12–24 months. **The companies that win at this stage are the ones that execute faster — and execution speed is a people and systems problem.**
