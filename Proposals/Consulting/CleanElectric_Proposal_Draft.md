# Consulting Proposal — Clean Electric
### Building the People Architecture for a Scaling EV Battery Company

---

## About the Client

**Clean Electric** is a Pune-based battery technology company founded in 2020, focused on advanced lithium-ion battery solutions for electric vehicles and stationary energy storage. In under 5 years, they have deployed **6,000+ batteries**, covered **20+ million kilometers**, served **12+ customers**, and expanded to **100+ deployment locations** across India.

Their product portfolio spans 2-wheelers (INSTA Series), 3 & 4-wheelers (FLO Series), ultra-fast charging (MACH Series), and premium performance batteries (0 Kelvin™ Series) — with industry-differentiating tech like immersion cooling, cell-to-pack architecture, and 4G telematics.

They are in active growth mode — hiring experienced professionals (5–10 years) across Manufacturing Engineering and Software Engineering.

---

## The Opportunity

Clean Electric is a high-growth, mission-driven startup that has built impressive product and deployment scale in a short time. But like most startups at this stage, **their people infrastructure has likely not kept pace with their product and business growth.**

This is the classic scale-up tension: the informal practices that worked at 10 people start to create friction at 30–50+. Without a structured people foundation — clear roles, defined KPIs, consistent performance management, and a rational compensation framework — growth becomes harder, retention suffers, and top talent gets frustrated.

This is exactly where Perfect Skills comes in.

---

## Identified Challenges (Inferred from Research)

| Area | Likely Pain Point |
|---|---|
| **Performance Management** | No standardized KPI framework across engineering, manufacturing, and software teams |
| **Goal Setting** | Goals likely set informally by individual managers, not cascaded from org strategy |
| **Role Clarity** | Rapid hiring without structured role definitions leads to overlap and ambiguity |
| **Compensation** | No defined pay bands — compensation likely decided case-by-case, creating internal inequity |
| **Designations** | Titles may have evolved organically, causing inconsistency and title inflation |
| **Leadership Pipeline** | No visible leadership development or middle management capability building |
| **Retention** | Without career ladders and structured growth paths, retaining experienced hires becomes a risk |

---

## Proposed Solution

### Engagement: People Architecture for Scale

A structured, phased consulting engagement to give Clean Electric the people infrastructure it needs to grow from a fast-moving startup to a high-performance organization.

---

### Workstream 1 — KPI & Goal Setting Workshop
**Format:** 2-Day Facilitated Leadership Workshop
**Audience:** Engineering leads, Manufacturing leads, Team Managers

Building a shared performance language across Clean Electric's technical and operational teams.

- What makes a strong KPI: alignment, measurability, ownership, review cadence
- SMART Goal Writing — with Clean Electric-specific examples (manufacturing yield, battery deployment, software release cycles)
- OKR introduction and cascade methodology — from company goals to team-level targets
- Goal Review Rhythm Guide — weekly, monthly, and quarterly cadences
- KPI Maturity Checklist — team-level self-assessment

**Outcome:** Every manager leaves with a working KPI framework and a goal-setting practice they can implement immediately.

---

### Workstream 2 — Strategic Talent Architecture Blueprint
**Format:** Consulting Document
**Audience:** Leadership & HR

Role design and KPI frameworks for Clean Electric's key positions — benchmarked against high-performing hardware and software companies.

- KPI frameworks for key roles: Manufacturing Engineering Lead, Software Engineering Lead, Product/R&D Lead
- Leadership Competency Matrix aligned to Clean Electric's growth stage and values
- Role-level accountability mapping with weighted responsibility areas
- Benchmarked against best practices in EV, hardware, and tech startup talent architecture

**Outcome:** A reusable reference document for hiring, performance management, promotion, and succession decisions.

---

### Workstream 3 — Compensation & Designation Framework
**Format:** HR Reference Document + Pay Structure
**Audience:** HR & Founding Team

Given Clean Electric is actively hiring experienced professionals at 5–10 years, getting compensation and designations right is critical for both attraction and retention.

- **Pay Band Design** — market-benchmarked pay ranges across levels and functions (Engineering, Manufacturing, Software, Sales, Operations)
- **Designation Rationalization** — a clean, unified title hierarchy from Associate → Lead → Manager → Senior Manager → Director
- **Career Ladder** — transparent growth paths for employees across each function
- **Variable Pay Guidelines** — linking performance ratings to incentive and increment decisions
- **Market Positioning** — benchmarked to EV/cleantech sector to keep Clean Electric competitive in talent acquisition

**Outcome:** Clean Electric can make fair, consistent, and defensible hiring and compensation decisions — and give employees a clear picture of their growth path.

---

### Workstream 4 (Optional) — 3-Month Post-Engagement Coaching
**Format:** Ongoing Engagement
**Audience:** Managers & Team Leads

Following the workshop and framework delivery, Perfect Skills can work directly with Clean Electric's managers to embed the frameworks into practice — coaching on KPI setting challenges, reviewing goal progress, and ensuring behavioral change sticks.
