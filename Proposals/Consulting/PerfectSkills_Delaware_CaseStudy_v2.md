## The Business Challenge

As Delaware India scaled its delivery teams, leadership faced a common but critical challenge: **inconsistent performance management practices across business units**. KPIs were being defined differently across teams, goal-setting lacked structure, and there was no unified framework connecting individual performance to organizational strategy.

The specific gaps identified:

- No standardized KPI design methodology across delivery units
- Limited use of OKR frameworks for cascading strategic goals
- Capability Leads and BU Heads lacked a shared competency language
- Performance reviews were activity-focused rather than outcome-driven
- Leadership bench development was ad hoc, with no structured pipeline visibility

Delaware India needed more than a generic training — they needed a **consultative, customized, and practically grounded capability-building engagement**.

## Approach & Methodology

Perfect Skills followed a structured, consultative approach across four phases:

### Phase 1 — Discovery & Alignment
The engagement began with a detailed discovery call between Perfect Skills and Delaware India's leadership team. The objective was to align on expectations, understand the participant profile, and map Delaware's existing performance culture. A mutual NDA was executed to ensure confidentiality of organizational data and materials.

### Phase 2 — Needs Analysis & Customization
Post-discovery, Perfect Skills sought inputs on:
- Organizational hierarchy and reporting structure
- Participant count, departments, and functional mix
- Existing KPI frameworks or performance tools in use
- Interest in post-training evaluation and follow-up sessions

This enabled the design of industry-specific and function-specific KPI examples directly relevant to Delaware's consulting and delivery context.

### Phase 3 — Program Design & Delivery
The two-day workshop was structured for maximum engagement and application:

**Session 1**
- What makes a strong KPI: Alignment, measurability, ownership, and review cadence
- KPI Design Reference Sheet with structure templates
- SMART Goal Writing — hands-on exercises with Delaware-specific examples
- OKR introduction and cascade methodology

**Session 2**
- KPI Maturity Checklist — team-level self-assessment
- Goal Review Rhythm Guide — weekly, monthly, and quarterly cadences
- 10 KPI Insights for Strategic Leaders
- Action planning and accountability mapping

### Phase 4 — Supporting Deliverables
Alongside the workshop, Perfect Skills delivered two high-value consulting documents:

**Strategic Talent Architecture Blueprint** — benchmarked against Google's OKR-led agility, LinkedIn's NPS-driven governance, and Infosys's predictive delivery practices, this document outlined:
- KPI frameworks for Director – Delivery Excellence (9 KPIs with global targets)
- KPI frameworks for BU Head – Strategic Delivery (13 KPIs with global targets)
- Leadership Competency Matrix across six dimensions
- Strategic alignment of Delaware's core values (Entrepreneurship, Care, Commitment, Respect, Teamwork) with behavioral leadership indicators

**Capability Lead Roles & Responsibilities Framework** — a weighted accountability structure covering:
- Capability Strategy & Roadmap (30%)
- Talent Development (25%)
- Knowledge Sharing (15%)
- Governance & Quality (15%)
- Reporting & Operational Oversight (15%)

### Phase 5 — Six-Month Growth & Development Engagement
Beyond the workshop, Perfect Skills committed to a **six-month focused engagement** with the participants. The trainer worked directly with Capability Leads and BU Heads to track the application of KPI frameworks, coach on goal-setting challenges, and drive measurable individual and team development. This extended partnership ensured that learning translated into sustained behavioral change — not just a one-time training event.
