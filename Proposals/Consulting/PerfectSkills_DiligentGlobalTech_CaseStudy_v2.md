# Designing for Scale
## Leadership Performance & Compensation Architecture for Diligent Global Tech
### A Case Study by Perfect Skills

## Executive Summary

Diligent Global Tech, a fast-growing technology services company, engaged Perfect Skills to address two critical organizational challenges simultaneously: **building a performance management culture** and **rationalizing its compensation and designation framework**. Perfect Skills designed and delivered a two-day KPI & Goal Setting Workshop for leaders and managers, co-developed a Strategic Talent Architecture Blueprint for key roles, and built a structured Pay Band and Designation Framework aligned to market benchmarks and internal equity. The outcome: a professionally structured, growth-ready people architecture that gave Diligent Global Tech the foundation to attract, retain, and develop talent with clarity and confidence.

## About the Client — Diligent Global Tech

Diligent Global Tech is a technology services and solutions company delivering software development, digital transformation, and managed IT services to clients across domestic and international markets. With a growing team spread across engineering, delivery, sales, and support functions, the company had reached a stage where informal people management practices were beginning to create friction — in hiring, retention, and team performance.

Leadership recognized the need for a structured intervention — one that would address performance management and compensation architecture in an integrated, coherent way.

**Key Stakeholders:**
- Managing Director & Leadership Team
- HR & People Operations
- Business Unit and Team Leads

---

## The Business Challenge

Diligent Global Tech's rapid growth had outpaced its internal people infrastructure. Two sets of challenges had become increasingly urgent:

### Performance Management Gaps
- No standardized KPI framework across engineering, delivery, and support functions
- Goal-setting was informal and inconsistent — driven by individual managers, not organizational strategy
- Team leads lacked tools to differentiate high performers from average contributors
- OKR and SMART goal-writing skills were absent at the middle-management layer
- Performance reviews were reactive and subjective rather than data-driven

### Compensation & Designation Confusion
- Designations had evolved organically — leading to title inflation, inconsistency, and internal inequity
- No defined pay bands or grade structure existed across levels and functions
- Compensation decisions were made on a case-by-case basis, creating disparities that affected morale and retention
- The absence of a structured career ladder made it difficult to set expectations during hiring or promotion conversations
- HR had no defensible framework to benchmark salaries against industry standards

The company needed a partner who could address both dimensions together — not in silos.

## Approach & Methodology

Perfect Skills followed a structured, consultative approach across four phases:

### Phase 1 — Discovery & Alignment
The engagement opened with a comprehensive discovery session with Diligent Global Tech's leadership and HR team. The goal was to map the current organizational structure, understand the participant profile for the workshop, and assess the existing state of designations and compensation. A mutual NDA ensured full confidentiality across all shared materials and data.

### Phase 2 — Needs Analysis & Customization
Perfect Skills conducted a structured needs analysis to gather inputs on:
- Current organizational hierarchy and headcount by function
- Existing titles, grades, and compensation ranges in practice
- Market positioning aspirations — whether to lead, match, or lag the market on pay
- Key roles to be covered under the KPI and talent architecture framework
- Participant mix for the workshop — functions, seniority, and team composition

This deep-dive ensured every deliverable was calibrated to Diligent Global Tech's specific context — not a generic industry template.

### Phase 3 — KPI & Goal Setting Workshop
The two-day workshop was designed to build a shared performance language across Diligent Global Tech's management layer:

**Session 1 — Building the KPI Foundation**
- What makes a strong KPI: Alignment, measurability, ownership, and review cadence
- KPI Design Reference Sheet with structure templates adapted for tech and delivery functions
- SMART Goal Writing — hands-on exercises with Diligent-specific examples
- OKR introduction and cascade methodology — from organizational goals to team-level targets

**Session 2 — Embedding Performance Culture**
- KPI Maturity Checklist — team-level self-assessment
- Goal Review Rhythm Guide — weekly, monthly, and quarterly cadences
- 10 KPI Insights for Strategic Leaders
- Action planning, ownership mapping, and accountability frameworks

### Phase 4 — Compensation & Designation Architecture
This was the defining differentiator of the Diligent Global Tech engagement — and where Perfect Skills added the most structural, long-term value.

**Pay Structure Design**
- Defined pay bands across all employee levels — from Associate to Senior Leadership
- Benchmarked compensation ranges against industry data for the IT services and technology sector
- Established internal equity principles ensuring pay consistency within grades
- Designed variable pay guidelines — linking performance ratings to incentive structures
- Created a pay progression model to guide increment and promotion-linked decisions

**Designation Rationalization Framework**
- Audited all existing designations across functions and identified overlaps, gaps, and title inflation
- Designed a clean, unified designation hierarchy with clear level definitions — from Associate through Consultant, Lead, Manager, and Director tiers
- Mapped each designation to a corresponding pay band, role complexity level, and career milestone
- Built a career ladder visual — giving employees a transparent view of growth paths within the organization
- Aligned designations with industry norms to support external talent acquisition and competitive positioning
