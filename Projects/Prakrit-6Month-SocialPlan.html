Prakrit AI — 6-Month Social Media Plan

May 2026 – October 2026

Overview

Details
BrandPrakrit AI
StagePost-launch, growth phase
Baseline20K users, 75%+ retention, PrakritGPT live
GoalBuild authority, grow community, drive user acquisition
Primary PlatformsLinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter)
Secondary PlatformsWhatsApp Broadcast, YouTube Shorts
Posting Frequency4–5 posts/week per platform
ToneWarm, human, trustworthy, educational — never clinical

Content Pillars (Domains)

These are the 6 core themes that every post should fall under:

PillarDescription% of Content
Health EducationExplain medical terms, reports, conditions in plain language30%
Product & FeaturesShowcase Prakrit AI capabilities with real use cases20%
Family & CaregivingStories, tips, and insights around family health management20%
Founder & Brand VoiceShailesh’s personal perspective, behind-the-scenes, BuildInPublic15%
Community & EngagementPolls, questions, user stories, feedback loops10%
Industry & TrendsHealthTech news, AI in healthcare, India health data5%

Post Types

TypePurposeFrequency
Educational carouselExplain a report, term, or health concept2x/week
Milestone / metric postShare growth numbers, retention, engagement1x/month
User storyReal or anonymised user experience1x/week
Product spotlightOne feature, explained simply1x/week
Founder voiceShailesh’s opinion, story, or reflection1x/week
Engagement postPoll, question, fill-in-the-blank2x/week
News / trend reactionReact to a health news item or research finding1x/week
Teaser / launch postBuild anticipation for upcoming featuresAs needed

Month 1 — May 2026: Momentum & Credibility

Theme: Ride the PrakritGPT wave. Establish authority. Convert attention into followers.

Goals

  • Capitalise on PrakritGPT launch buzz
  • Build social proof through user stories and metrics
  • Establish Prakrit AI as the go-to family health AI in India

Weekly Focus

WeekFocus
Week 120K celebration content, user love posts, retention story
Week 2PrakritGPT deep dive — what it does, how it works, why it matters
Week 3Health literacy series — “What does this mean?” (common report terms)
Week 4Founder reflection — Shailesh on the journey, vision, and what’s next

Content Topics

  • “What your CBC report is actually telling you”
  • “Why 75% of our users come back every week”
  • “How PrakritGPT answered questions a doctor didn’t have time to”
  • “5 medical terms every Indian family should know”
  • “The moment we knew Prakrit AI was working” (founder post)

Research Angles

  • Health literacy gap in India — only 12% of Indians fully understand their medical reports (FICCI Health Report)
  • Rise of AI in Indian healthcare — NASSCOM report on digital health adoption
  • Average family spends 3+ hours/month managing health paperwork (internal insight)

Article References

  • FICCI-EY Health Report 2024 — “Future of Healthcare in India”
  • NASSCOM 2025 — “AI Adoption in Indian Healthcare”
  • WHO — “Health Literacy and Patient Outcomes”
  • Lancet India — “Chronic Disease Burden in Indian Families”

Month 2 — June 2026: Education & Awareness

Theme: Position Prakrit AI as the health education brand for Indian families.

Goals

  • Grow organic reach through high-value educational content
  • Build trust with audiences who haven’t heard of Prakrit AI
  • Drive signups from health-curious users

Weekly Focus

WeekFocus
Week 1Common blood test values explained (Haemoglobin, TSH, Vitamin D, B12)
Week 2Diabetes awareness — understanding HbA1c, fasting glucose, and what they mean
Week 3Heart health — cholesterol panels, BP readings, ECG basics
Week 4Monsoon health prep — what to test before the season, what to watch for

Content Topics

  • “Your Vitamin D report decoded” (carousel)
  • “HbA1c explained in 60 seconds”
  • “Why your cholesterol report has 4 numbers and what each means”
  • “Pre-monsoon health checklist for your family”
  • “What a ‘normal’ range actually means on a lab report”
  • “Ask PrakritGPT: real questions our users asked this week”

Research Angles

  • Vitamin D deficiency affects 70–90% of Indians (AIIMS study)
  • India has 77 million diabetics — second highest globally (IDF Atlas 2023)
  • Monsoon season drives 30% spike in vector-borne disease consultations

Article References

  • IDF Diabetes Atlas 2023 — India statistics
  • AIIMS Study — “Vitamin D Deficiency Prevalence in Urban India”
  • ICMR — “Normal Reference Ranges for Indian Population”
  • Journal of Family Medicine — “Preventive Health Screening in Indian Households”

Month 3 — July 2026: Community & Stories

Theme: Let the users speak. Build emotional connection. Make the brand feel human.

Goals

  • Deepen community engagement
  • Generate UGC (user generated content) and testimonials
  • Increase shares and word-of-mouth referrals

Weekly Focus

WeekFocus
Week 1User story series launch — “A family, a report, a moment of clarity”
Week 2Caregiver appreciation — posts for those managing parents’ health
Week 3Community challenge — “Share your health win this month”
Week 4Midpoint review — what the community has taught us, product updates

Content Topics

  • “She uploaded her father’s kidney report at midnight. Here’s what happened.”
  • “The caregiver’s burden — and how we’re trying to lighten it”
  • “What 10,000 conversations with PrakritGPT taught us about Indian health anxiety”
  • “Your health wins — a community thread”
  • “We asked 500 users what they wish they’d known about their health sooner”
  • “Behind the product: how we decide what to build next”

Research Angles

  • 60% of Indian caregivers report moderate to severe stress (Alzheimer’s & Related Disorders Society of India)
  • Women are primary health managers in 72% of Indian households
  • Mental health impact of health anxiety in families — emerging research

Article References

  • ARDSI — “Caregiver Burden in India Report”
  • Pew Research — “Family Health Management Roles Globally”
  • BMJ — “Health Anxiety and Digital Health Tools”
  • Economic Times — “The sandwich generation — managing parents and children’s health”

Month 4 — August 2026: Feature Deep Dives

Theme: Educate users on what Prakrit AI can actually do. Convert awareness into activation.

Goals

  • Drive feature adoption among existing users
  • Reduce churn by showing underused features
  • Position Prakrit AI as comprehensive, not just a chatbot

Weekly Focus

WeekFocus
Week 1Family Profiles — the most underrated feature we built
Week 2Health Timeline — your entire health story in one scroll
Week 3Medication Tracking — never miss a dose again
Week 4Emergency Health Card — the feature you hope you never need

Content Topics

  • “One account. Your whole family’s health history.” (Family Profiles spotlight)
  • “What if you could scroll through 5 years of your health in 30 seconds?” (Timeline)
  • “Medication non-adherence costs India ₹35,000 crore/year. We built a fix.” (Medication Tracking)
  • “Your emergency card could save your life. Here’s how to set it up.” (Emergency Card)
  • “The 5 things Prakrit AI can do that most users haven’t discovered yet”
  • “Voice mode — talk to your health assistant, literally”

Research Angles

  • Medication non-adherence in India — 50–75% of patients don’t complete prescribed courses
  • Emergency preparedness — less than 5% of Indians carry critical health information
  • India’s ageing population — 140 million seniors by 2030 (Census projections)

Article References

  • WHO — “Medication Adherence: The Silent Epidemic”
  • Indian Journal of Medical Research — “Prescription Non-Adherence in India”
  • HelpAge India — “Ageing in India Report 2024”
  • Times of India Health — “Emergency Preparedness in Urban India”

Month 5 — September 2026: WhatsApp Launch & Growth Push

Theme: PrakritGPT comes to WhatsApp. Make it a cultural moment.

Goals

  • Drive massive awareness around WhatsApp launch
  • Acquire new users through WhatsApp’s network effect
  • Generate press and organic buzz

Weekly Focus

WeekFocus
Week 1Pre-launch teaser campaign — “Something big is coming to your WhatsApp”
Week 2Launch day — full push across all platforms
Week 3How-to content — getting started with PrakritGPT on WhatsApp
Week 4User reactions & early stories from WhatsApp users

Content Topics

  • “You asked. We listened. PrakritGPT is coming to WhatsApp.” (teaser)
  • “Launch day: PrakritGPT is now on WhatsApp 🎉”
  • “How to talk to your AI health assistant on WhatsApp — a 3-step guide”
  • “Already got 10,000 WhatsApp messages in 24 hours. Here’s what people are asking.”
  • “Why WhatsApp? Because health conversations should happen where life happens.”
  • “PrakritGPT on WhatsApp: what it can and can’t do (be honest post)“

Research Angles

  • WhatsApp has 500M+ users in India — largest market globally
  • 78% of Indians prefer WhatsApp for receiving health information (GSMA report)
  • Conversational AI on messaging platforms — adoption curve research

Article References

  • GSMA Intelligence — “Mobile Internet in India 2025”
  • Meta — “WhatsApp Business India Report”
  • Stanford HAI — “Conversational AI in Low-Resource Settings”
  • Indian Express — “How WhatsApp became India’s primary health information channel”

Month 6 — October 2026: Authority & Scale

Theme: Establish Prakrit AI as the thought leader in family health AI in India.

Goals

  • Build long-term brand authority
  • Attract partnerships, press, and investor attention
  • Set up for year-2 growth

Weekly Focus

WeekFocus
Week 16-month retrospective — growth, learnings, surprises
Week 2Thought leadership — “The future of healthcare in India is personal”
Week 3Roadmap teaser — what’s coming in 2027
Week 4Community celebration — user milestones, gratitude, next chapter

Content Topics

  • “6 months. 20,000 families. Here’s what we learned.” (milestone post)
  • “AI will not replace doctors. It will give families the confidence to talk to them.”
  • “What India’s healthcare system gets wrong — and what families can do about it”
  • “The 3 biggest mistakes people make when managing family health records”
  • “Here’s our product roadmap for 2027. Tell us what you think.”
  • “To every family that trusted us with their health — thank you.”

Research Angles

  • India’s digital health market projected at $10.6B by 2025 (IBEF)
  • Doctor-patient ratio in India: 1:834 (WHO recommends 1:1000)
  • AI-assisted healthcare can reduce diagnostic errors by 20% — Stanford Medicine

Article References

  • IBEF — “Healthcare Industry in India 2025”
  • WHO — “Global Health Workforce Report”
  • Stanford Medicine — “AI in Clinical Decision Support”
  • NITI Aayog — “National Digital Health Blueprint”
  • McKinsey — “The Future of AI in Healthcare”

Platform-Specific Notes

LinkedIn

  • Long-form founder posts perform best
  • Data-driven posts (stats, metrics) get high reach
  • Shailesh’s personal account should post 3x/week alongside brand page
  • Focus: thought leadership, product milestones, team culture

Instagram

  • Carousels for education (save rate = algorithm boost)
  • Reels for quick health tips and behind-the-scenes
  • Stories for polls, Q&A, daily engagement
  • Focus: visual health education, user stories, feature demos

X / Twitter

  • Quick takes on health news
  • Engage with health journalists and doctors
  • Thread format for complex topics
  • Focus: real-time engagement, trend-jacking, industry conversations

WhatsApp Broadcast (post-September)

  • Weekly health tips
  • Feature updates
  • Exclusive content for subscribers
