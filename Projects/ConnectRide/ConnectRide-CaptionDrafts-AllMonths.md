### Day 25 — Administrator Guide
**Type:** Post | **Platform:** Facebook

For school administrators and district transportation coordinators — here's a guide to evaluating any transportation provider before you sign a contract.

**6 Questions to Ask Before Choosing a School Transportation Partner:**

1. How are drivers recruited, screened, and trained?
2. What technology do you use for real-time tracking and communication?
3. How do you handle incidents or deviations from planned routes?
4. What does your reporting and compliance documentation look like?
5. How do you communicate with parents — and how quickly?
6. What happens when a vehicle breaks down or a driver doesn't show up?
