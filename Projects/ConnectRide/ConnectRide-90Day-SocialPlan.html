Connect Ride — 90-Day Social Media Plan

Instagram & Facebook | Awareness → Leads → Signups

Overview

Brand: Connect Ride LLC Tagline: Safe, Smart School Transportation Tone: Community-driven and professional Market: United States Platforms: Instagram + Facebook Goals: Brand Awareness → Lead Generation → Signups

Target Audience

AudienceWho They AreWhat They Care About
ParentsParents of K-12 studentsSafety, reliability, real-time visibility, peace of mind
Schools & AdministratorsPrincipals, transport coordinators, district officialsCost efficiency, accountability, compliance, technology
School DistrictsDecision-makers in student transportationScalability, data, safety records, vendor reliability

Content Pillars

Every post falls under one of five pillars:

PillarThemePurpose
🛡️ Safety FirstChild safety, driver vetting, trackingBuild trust with parents and schools
💡 Smart TechnologyAI, real-time tracking, fleet managementDifferentiate from traditional transportation
🤝 CommunityParents, schools, drivers, local storiesBuild emotional connection and loyalty
📚 EducationTips, guides, school transport best practicesPosition as an authority, provide value
📣 Social Proof & MilestonesWins, partnerships, testimonialsDrive credibility and FOMO

Platform Strategy

Instagram

  • Feed Posts: 4x per week — high-quality visuals, carousels, infographics (Canva)
  • Stories: Daily — polls, Q&As, behind-the-scenes, quick tips
  • Focus: Visual storytelling, community engagement, reach

Facebook

  • Posts: 4x per week — mirrored or adapted from Instagram
  • Facebook Groups: Engage in local parent and school district groups
  • Events: Promote any webinars, demos, or open days
  • Facebook Ads: Boost high-performing organic posts for lead gen (from Month 2)
  • Focus: Community building, longer-form content, lead generation via forms

Quick Execution Tips

  • Canva — create branded templates once (colors, fonts, logo), reuse across all posts
  • Perplexity — use for research-backed captions, industry stats, and content ideas
  • Stock visuals — Unsplash and Pexels for school bus, children, community, and technology imagery
  • Consistency over perfection — post on schedule even if the content isn’t perfect; algorithms reward consistency
  • Engage back — reply to every comment and DM within 24 hours, especially in Month 1
  • Facebook Groups — join local school parent groups and administrator groups; add value before mentioning Connect Ride
  • UTM links — add UTM parameters to every link (connectridellc.com?utm_source=instagram) to track which platform drives signups
