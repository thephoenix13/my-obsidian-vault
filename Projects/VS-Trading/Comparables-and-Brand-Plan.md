# VS Trading — Comparables & Brand / Social Plan

> Context: Companies to study as we plan VS Trading's launch (Jan 2027), plus a presentability and social media playbook. Both the comparables and the social plan are **lane-dependent** — what counts as "similar" and what's permissible to say publicly changes drastically between a SEBI PMS (Lane A), an AIF Cat III (Lane B), and a proprietary trading firm (Lane C). See [[Cofounder-Alignment-Framework]] for the lane decision framework.

---

## Two upfront caveats

1. **Comparables vary by lane** — the firms to study look different depending on whether VS Trading lands as PMS, AIF, or prop. All three lists below.
2. **SEBI's advertisement code restricts regulated entities sharply** — the current pitch deck's tone (5-8x FD returns, emoji plans, "MOST POPULAR" badges) would be a compliance violation the day VS Trading registers. The social plan below is the regulated-firm playbook, because that's the version that survives.

## Presentability & social media plan

### Brand foundation — the "fix this first" list

Before any social media push, the existing brand has problems that would carry forward:

| Current state | What needs to change |
|---|---|
| Pitch deck full of emojis, gradients, "MOST POPULAR" badges | Institutional-grade visual identity — clean typography, restrained palette, no emojis in collateral |
| "5-8x more than Bank FD," "36-60% annually" | Performance claims removed entirely or replaced with SEBI-format track record (post-registration) |
| "Where Markets Meet Mastery" tagline | Workable but tired. Worth testing alternatives once positioning is locked. |
| Gmail-based founder email (`vstrading2712@gmail.com`) | Domain email (vinayak@vstrading.in or similar). Gmail signals informal/retail. |
| WhatsApp as primary channel in deck | Demote — WhatsApp is fine for client service, not for marketing. |
| Generic founder photo placeholder | Professional photography across all four cofounders |
| No website mentioned | Website is non-negotiable before any social push |

### Content pillars (works for Lane A or B; skip if Lane C)

Four pillars, balanced roughly 30/30/25/15:

1. **Process content (30%)** — How we think, not what we trade. Research framework, risk philosophy, position sizing principles. This is where Marcellus and 2Point2 win — they teach their philosophy publicly.
2. **Market commentary (30%)** — Macro views, sector reads, themes. Educational, not predictive. No "buy X" calls (that's Research Analyst-licensed territory).
3. **Founder credibility (25%)** — Vinayak's journey, the engineering-to-trading angle, lessons from drawdowns. Long-form is fine — LinkedIn articles, Medium-style posts.
4. **Firm transparency (15%)** — Quarterly letters (PMS-mandated anyway), team additions, milestones. Build the "real firm" perception over time.

### Platform strategy

| Platform | Priority | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Tier 1 | HNI prospects, intermediary distributors, advisor network all live here | Founder-led posts > corporate page. All four cofounders post in their own voice. |
| **Twitter/X** | Tier 1 | Indian "fintwit" is the most active investing community. Samir Arora, FinFloww, Nithin Kamath all built here. | Vinayak's account is the asset. Daily presence, no automation, real engagement. |
| **YouTube** | Tier 2 | Long-form credibility-building. Quarterly investor letters as video. Vinayak's process explained. | Don't start until Tier 1 is rhythmic. YouTube needs editing budget. |
| **Website + blog** | Tier 1 (foundational) | Owned property, SEO, primary source of truth | Should exist before any social push |
| **Podcast appearances** | Tier 2 | Better ROI than starting our own podcast in year 1 | Vinayak on FinFloww, WTF, Mint, etc. |
| **Instagram** | Skip | Wrong audience for HNI/PMS clients | Revisit only if pivoting to retail RIA |
| **Telegram** | Avoid | Reputationally tainted in Indian financial services — associated with tip scammers and unregulated calls | Don't go here even for legitimate uses |
| **WhatsApp Channels/Business** | Tier 3 | Useful for client servicing, not acquisition | Post-launch, regulated use only |

### The hard "don't do this" list (compliance-driven)

Once VS Trading is SEBI-registered, every one of these is a violation:

- No guaranteed returns claims, ever
- No comparisons to other asset classes that aren't risk-adjusted
- No client testimonials (regulated)
- No performance numbers outside SEBI's mandated disclosure format
- No "limited time" or "exclusive opportunity" urgency framing
- No screenshots of P&L or account statements
- No DMs offering investment opportunities (cold outreach)
- No founder posts that look like trade calls ("I'm buying X today")
- No engagement with unregistered "advisors" or amplifying their content

### A first-12-months cadence (post-registration, assuming Lane A or B)

- **Months 1–2:** Website live, founder LinkedIn profiles polished, first three pieces of process content published. Zero promotion.
- **Months 3–4:** Founder LinkedIn rhythm (2 posts/week from Vinayak, 1/week from each of Pratik/Aashish/Shashi). Twitter account live for Vinayak.
- **Months 5–6:** First quarterly investor letter published publicly (anonymized examples). First podcast appearance secured.
- **Months 7–9:** Content library at 30+ pieces. Outbound to allocators, family offices, distributors.
- **Months 10–12:** Optional YouTube channel launch with quarterly letter video. Speaking slot at a credible event (CFA Society India, Moneycontrol Forum).

**Budget reality:** A credible launch presence runs ₹15–25L in year one (website, design, photography, content production, PR retainer if used). Cheaper is possible but slower and more amateur-looking.
