## Brand & presentability plan

### Brand foundation for the new firm

The launch of an incorporated, SEBI-aware firm calls for an updated brand identity that signals institutional credibility to HNI clients, family offices, and distributors. Priorities:

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Visual identity | Institutional-grade — clean typography, restrained palette, professional photography of all four founders |
| Tagline & positioning | Test alternatives once final lane is locked; signal trust and process over performance promises |
| Founder emails | Domain-based (e.g., `vinayak@vstrading.in`) — signals "real firm" |
| Website | Foundational — primary source of truth, must exist before any social push |
| Collateral (deck, one-pager, brochure) | Aligned with the new visual identity and regulatory-compliant language |
| Photography | Professional headshots and team imagery for all four cofounders |

### Content pillars (Lane A or B)

Four content pillars, balanced roughly 30/30/25/15:

1. **Process content (30%)** — How we think and operate. Research framework, risk philosophy, position sizing principles. This is where Marcellus and 2Point2 win — they teach their philosophy publicly.
2. **Market commentary (30%)** — Macro views, sector reads, themes. Educational, not predictive. No specific trade calls (that's Research Analyst-licensed territory).
3. **Founder credibility (25%)** — Vinayak's journey, the engineering-to-trading angle, lessons from market cycles. Long-form welcomes — LinkedIn articles, blog posts.
4. **Firm transparency (15%)** — Quarterly letters (PMS-mandated anyway), team additions, milestones. Build the "real firm" perception over time.

### Platform strategy

### Compliance guardrails for content (post-registration)

Once VS Trading is SEBI-registered, the advertisement code applies to every public communication. Internalize these:

- No guaranteed-return claims
- No comparisons to other asset classes that aren't risk-adjusted
- No client testimonials (regulated)
- No performance numbers outside SEBI's mandated disclosure format
- No "limited time" or "exclusive opportunity" urgency framing
- No screenshots of P&L or account statements
- No DMs offering investment opportunities (cold outreach)
- No founder posts that look like trade calls
- No engagement with unregistered "advisors" or amplifying their content

### First-12-months cadence (post-registration, Lane A or B)

- **Months 1–2:** Website live, founder LinkedIn profiles polished, first three pieces of process content published.
- **Months 3–4:** Founder LinkedIn rhythm (2 posts/week from Vinayak, 1/week each from Pratik / Aashish / Shashi). Twitter account live for Vinayak.
- **Months 5–6:** First quarterly investor letter published. First podcast appearance secured.
- **Months 7–9:** Content library at 30+ pieces. Outbound to allocators, family offices, distributors.
- **Months 10–12:** YouTube channel launch with quarterly letter video. Speaking slot at a credible event (CFA Society India, Moneycontrol Forum).

### Year-one budget reality

A credible launch presence runs ₹15–25L in year one — website, design, photography, content production, PR retainer if used. Cheaper is possible but slower.
