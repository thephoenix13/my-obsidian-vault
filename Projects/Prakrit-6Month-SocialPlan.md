# Prakrit AI — 6-Month Social Media Plan
### May 2026 – October 2026

---

## Overview

| | Details |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Prakrit AI |
| **Stage** | Post-launch, growth phase |
| **Baseline** | 20K users, 75%+ retention, PrakritGPT live |
| **Goal** | Build authority, grow community, drive user acquisition |
| **Primary Platforms** | LinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter) |
| **Secondary Platforms** | WhatsApp Broadcast, YouTube Shorts |
| **Posting Frequency** | 4–5 posts/week per platform |
| **Tone** | Warm, human, trustworthy, educational — never clinical |

---

## Content Pillars (Domains)

These are the 6 core themes that every post should fall under:

| Pillar | Description | % of Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Health Education** | Explain medical terms, reports, conditions in plain language | 30% |
| **Product & Features** | Showcase Prakrit AI capabilities with real use cases | 20% |
| **Family & Caregiving** | Stories, tips, and insights around family health management | 20% |
| **Founder & Brand Voice** | Shailesh's personal perspective, behind-the-scenes, BuildInPublic | 15% |
| **Community & Engagement** | Polls, questions, user stories, feedback loops | 10% |
| **Industry & Trends** | HealthTech news, AI in healthcare, India health data | 5% |

---

## Post Types

| Type | Purpose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Educational carousel** | Explain a report, term, or health concept | 2x/week |
| **Milestone / metric post** | Share growth numbers, retention, engagement | 1x/month |
| **User story** | Real or anonymised user experience | 1x/week |
| **Product spotlight** | One feature, explained simply | 1x/week |
| **Founder voice** | Shailesh's opinion, story, or reflection | 1x/week |
| **Engagement post** | Poll, question, fill-in-the-blank | 2x/week |
| **News / trend reaction** | React to a health news item or research finding | 1x/week |
| **Teaser / launch post** | Build anticipation for upcoming features | As needed |

## Month 1 — May 2026: Momentum & Credibility

**Theme:** *Ride the PrakritGPT wave. Establish authority. Convert attention into followers.*

### Goals
- Capitalise on PrakritGPT launch buzz
- Build social proof through user stories and metrics
- Establish Prakrit AI as the go-to family health AI in India

### Weekly Focus
| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 20K celebration content, user love posts, retention story |
| Week 2 | PrakritGPT deep dive — what it does, how it works, why it matters |
| Week 3 | Health literacy series — "What does this mean?" (common report terms) |

### Content Topics
- "What your CBC report is actually telling you"

## Platform-Specific Notes

### LinkedIn
- Long-form founder posts perform best
- Data-driven posts (stats, metrics) get high reach
- Shailesh's personal account should post 3x/week alongside brand page
- Focus: thought leadership, product milestones, team culture

### Instagram
- Carousels for education (save rate = algorithm boost)
- Reels for quick health tips and behind-the-scenes
- Stories for polls, Q&A, daily engagement
- Focus: visual health education, user stories, feature demos

### X / Twitter
- Quick takes on health news
- Engage with health journalists and doctors
- Thread format for complex topics
- Focus: real-time engagement, trend-jacking, industry conversations

### WhatsApp Broadcast (post-September)
- Weekly health tips
- Feature updates
- Exclusive content for subscribers
