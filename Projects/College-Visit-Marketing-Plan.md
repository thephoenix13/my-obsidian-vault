# College Visit Marketing Plan — Prakrit AI

## Objective
Drive **student signups** for Prakrit AI across college visits spanning IT, Medical, and MBA institutions. Students manage their own health and their family's — Prakrit AI solves that for them.

---

## What We're Pitching

### Prakrit AI
A family health management and AI-powered health companion platform. Upload medical reports, track medications, get 24/7 AI health assistance — all in one place, for the whole family.

- **Primary audience:** Students across all streams
- **CTA:** Join the waitlist / Sign up free
- **Waitlist URL:** https://prakrit.ai/join-waitlist
- **Live proof:** 20,000+ users, 75%+ weekly repeat rate in 3 Months

---

## Why Students? The Core Insight

Most college students are:
- Living away from home for the first time
- Managing their own health independently (possibly for the first time)
- Responsible for keeping track of family health back home
- Confused by lab reports and prescriptions
- Not going to a doctor until something is seriously wrong

**Prakrit AI is exactly what they need — and most of them don't know it exists yet.**

## College Segmentation & Pitch Angle

### IT / Engineering Colleges

## On-Ground Execution Flow

### Before the Visit
- [ ] Identify college point of contact (student coordinator / placement officer)
- [ ] Prepare a 2–3 min live demo: show by demo login → show PrakritGPT explanation

### At the Visit (15–20 min) - Demo
1. **Hook (2 min)** — "When was the last time you actually understood a medical report? Or helped your parents understand one?"
2. **Problem framing (3 min)** — Scattered records, confusing reports, no health continuity between doctor visits
3. **Demo (7 min)** — Live: upload a report → PrakritGPT explains it in plain language. Show family profiles, medication tracking, WhatsApp assistant
4. **Social proof (1 min)** — 20,000 users, 75%+ come back every week, PrakritGPT launched 2 weeks ago with overwhelmingly positive response
5. **CTA (2 min)** — Scan the QR, sign up right now, it's free
6. **Q&A + collect contacts**

### After the Visit
- [ ] Follow up with the college contact within 24 hours (WhatsApp message with signup link)
- [ ] Send a short message to interested students who didn't sign up on the spot
- [ ] Log the visit: college, date, stream, signups collected, follow-up status

## Signup Funnel

```
Student hears pitch / sees demo
          ↓
Scans QR / visits link
          ↓
Joins waitlist or signs up on Prakrit AI
          ↓
Onboarded → uploads first document or sets up family profile
```

- Keep signup frictionless — name, email, phone is enough
- Use **college-specific QR codes or referral codes** to measure which visits convert
